# Legal basis and regulatory comparison

How the modelled process relates to Turkish legislation on workplace licences for
*sıhhi müessese* (non-hazardous premises such as shops, cafés and restaurants without
alcohol service). Short Turkish quotations are given in the original, with sources.

**Scope and date.** The model reflects the practice of a district municipality
(*ilçe belediyesi*) in Istanbul around 2014–2015, as implemented in its municipal
software. This comparison reflects the legislation as of November 2026. It is a process
analysis, not legal advice.

## Reading the process

The diagram is a **software workflow**: user tasks are screens or forms in the municipal
system (data entry, scanning documents such as the tax certificate, issuing printed
documents). Physical work happens outside the system and appears only where its start or
result is recorded.

In practice, businesses usually **opened first and applied afterwards**; the workflow starts
when the owner comes to the municipality. The process is therefore best read as:

**declaration → (business operating) → finalising inspection → final licence, or cancellation and closure**

## Who is competent

Inside a metropolitan municipality, the district municipality licenses *sıhhi* premises
(Law 3572, Art. 3): "Sıhhi ve sair işletmeler için ise büyükşehir belediyesi içinde kalan
diğer belediye başkanlıkları." ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/1989/06/17/3572-sayili-kanun-isyeri-acma/))

## Step-by-step comparison

| Process element | Legal basis | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application with declaration form (Stage 1) | Regulation 2005/9207, Art. 6: a receipt (*Örnek 6 alındı belgesi*) showing the officer, date and time is issued for every application ([source](https://www.hukukihaber.net/danistay-4-dairenin-20237271-e-20237311-k-sayili-karari)). Art. 12: if the application meets the criteria, the licence is issued "aynı gün içinde" ([source](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=21&MevzuatNo=20059207&MevzuatTertip=5)). | Consistent. The receipt and the same-day declaration-based licence are not drawn in the source; added as a note. |
| Business already operating | Art. 6: "İşyeri açma ve çalışma ruhsatı alınmadan açılan işyerleri yetkili idareler tarafından kapatılır." | As-is practice departs from the regulation. Recorded as a note, not "fixed" in the model. |
| "Business active?" check and tax accrual (Stage 1) | Environmental cleaning tax, Law 2464 rep. Art. 44: "Mükellefiyet binanın kullanımı ile başlar." ([source](https://www.yenisehir.bel.tr/sites/default/files/2019-06/ctv.pdf)) | Consistent; also evidence that the business is usually operating. |
| Fire report decision (Stage 2) | Art. 5: required only for certain premises (entertainment venues, flammable storage, more than 30 employees, certain multi-shop buildings) ([source](https://www.jmo.org.tr/mevzuat/mevzuat_detay_pdf.php?kod=212)). | Consistent: the gateway makes exactly this distinction. |
| Zoning status review (Stage 2a) | Zoning Law 3194, Art. 3: "Herhangi bir saha, her ölçekteki plan esaslarına … aykırı maksatlar için kullanılamaz." ([source](https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/yerelyonetimler/icerikler/zeyt-nyagi-uret-m-tes-s--20240705145712.pdf)) | Legally grounded. The two-branch review with chiefs, rapporteurs and approvals is internal practice. |
| Site inspection, 15 days, second inspection, cancellation (Stage 3) | Law 3572, Art. 6: premises are inspected within one month; otherwise "çalışma ruhsatı almış sayılır" ([source](https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/yerelyonetimler/icerikler/ruhsat--ptal--taleb--20220309150000.pdf)). Regulation Art. 13: "bir defaya mahsus olmak üzere onbeş günlük süre verilir. Verilen süre içinde tespit edilen noksanlık ve aykırılıklar giderilmediği takdirde, ruhsat iptal edilerek işyeri kapatılır." ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2015/03/10/isyeri-acma-ve-calisma-ruhsati-yargi-karari/)) | Consistent: this stage is the Art. 13 finalising inspection. The one-month limit is shown as a group on the overview; in practice earlier stages finish within days. |
| Licensing commission (Stage 4) | The inspection board of Art. 15 applies to first-class *gayrisıhhi* premises, not to *sıhhi* premises. | Internal practice, not required. |
| Fee accrual and collection (Stage 4) | Law 2464, Art. 81: opening a workplace is subject to the *İşyeri Açma İzni Harcı* ([source](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/ozelgeler/b-07-0-gel-0-66-6610-58-15906-sayili-ozelge-1)). | Consistent. |
| Deputy mayor and mayor approval (Stage 5) | Art. 6: the licence is signed by the head of the authority or a delegate; no council or committee decision is needed. | Lawful; two levels are internal practice. |
| Cancellation authority (Stage 5) | Council of State: cancellation is for the mayor or a delegate; the municipal committee cannot decide it ([source](https://www.hukukihaber.net/isyeri-ruhsatinin-iptali-veya-verilmemesi-durumlarinda-dava-ve-yurutmenin-durdurulmasi-yargi-kararlariyla/amp)). | Consistent. |
| Final licence (Stage 6) | Art. 13: the declaration-based licence becomes final after the inspection. | Consistent; noted on the "license" step. |
| Closure (Stage C, added) | Art. 13 ends with closure. Sealing requires a cancellation decision first; direct, indefinite sealing without it is unlawful ([source](https://www.hukukihaber.net/yangin-yonetmeligine-aykirilik-nedeniyle-ruhsat-iptali-ve-suresiz-men-yapilamayacagi-hakkinda-karar-degerlendirmesi-istanbul-bim-6-iddnin-09092025-tarihli-karari-isiginda)). | Not in the source; added as Stage C and marked in red. Appeals go to administrative court and are out of scope. |

## Changes since the process was drawn (2015 → 2026)

Articles 6, 12 and 13 — the core of this process — show no amendment since 2007. Changes
that would matter if the process were run today:

| Date | Change | Effect on this process |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 Jun 2020 (Decision 2626) | Professional competence: the owner or staff need a mastery certificate, relevant diploma or course certificate where such documents exist for the trade ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/06/09/isyeri-acma-ve-calisma-ruhsatlari-2626/)). | Add a document check at registration. |
| 17 Apr 2021 (Decision 3835) | Transfer rules: documents not submitted in time lead to cancellation and closure ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2021/04/17/isyeri-acma-ve-calisma-ruhsatlari-degisiklik-3835/)). | Transfers are out of scope. |
| 11 Dec 2025 (Decision 10681) | New Art. 45/A: if a compliant application is not licensed within two months, the Ministry of Environment, Urbanisation and Climate Change can issue it ex officio ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/12/11/isyeri-acma-ve-calisma-ruhsatlarina-iliskin-yonetmelikte-degisiklik-karar-sayisi-10681/)). | Track a two-month limit from the application date. |
| Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 | Lodging premises must hold a building use permit and fire report; failure leads to cancellation and closure ([source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/01/10/konaklama-tesislerinde-yangin-uygunlugu-itfaiye-raporu-sunulamazsa-ruhsat-iptali-ve-kapatma/amp/)). | Update the fire-report rule for lodging. |

Other amendments between 2017 and 2024 add sector-specific requirements (internet cafés,
estate agents, car dealers, valet services, jewellers, EV charging) or change the
*gayrisıhhi* lists, and do not affect this process.

**Limitation.** Amendments were reviewed through Official Gazette summaries and consolidated
texts; check the current text on [mevzuat.gov.tr](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=21&MevzuatNo=20059207&MevzuatTertip=5) before relying on it.
